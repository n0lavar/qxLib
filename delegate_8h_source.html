33  @class delegate
34  @brief Single or multicast delegate.
35  @details For a singlecast version:
36  1. Create with qx::delegate<...>::create_singlecast(...)
37  2. Call delegate.execute(...)
38  For a multicast version:
39  1. Default construct
40  2. All various callbacks with delegate.add_xxx(...)
41  2. Call delegate.execute(...)
42  @tparam signature_t - std::function-like signature of the delegate. For example: void(size_t, const qx::string&).
43  Is the result type satisfies qx::delegate_pipe_c,
44  the result of each callable will be piped into the next one,
45  and the final result will be returned by execute().
46  Otherwise, the result of the last callable will be returned by execute(),
47  and the results of the previous callables will be ignored.
